## For the love of the ride.

We are **ARDUUS**, a mountain bike equipment and tools brand. Riding inspires what we build: practical gear and useful tools that help riders get more from their time on the trail.

We believe capable equipment should be within reach of more riders. Our work brings together hardware and software, with a focus on thoughtful design, everyday usefulness, and value.

<br />

## Meet RAVOX

**Bike-powered lighting for Avinox-equipped bikes.**

RAVOX connects to your bike’s electrical system, keeping your lighting part of the bike. A handlebar remote puts brightness and beam control within reach, while the bike’s built-in controls handle on and off.

- **One less battery to charge.** Power comes directly from your bike.
- **Control from the bars.** Adjust the light as the trail changes.
- **A considered setup.** A dedicated mount keeps the light integrated with your cockpit.
